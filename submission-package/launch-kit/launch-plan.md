## 7. Key Risks & Concrete Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood / Impact | Concrete Mitigation Built Into This Engine |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Unverified Product Claims or AI Hallucinations Slip Into Copy** | Medium / Critical (Brand trust & assessment disqualification) | Automated pre-export check (`engine/factual_language_review.py` + `engine/asset_audit.py`) blocks guaranteed-outcome language, multiplier claims (`10x`), and unauthorized feature claims. All 30 launch assets teach general creator workflows without unverified product promises. |
| **2. Media Rights, Font Licensing, or Synthetic Disclosure Oversights** | Medium / High (Platform takedown or compliance failure) | `py -3.12 -m engine.rights_inventory` scans every HTML source and video asset (`launch-kit/rights-inventory.csv`). All 30 assets remain `rights_checked = No` and `status = Draft` until human sign-off; YouTube Shorts (`YT-01..10`) are explicitly flagged for YouTube's altered/synthetic content disclosure (`ai_label_needed = Yes`). |
| **3. Video Resolution or Aspect-Ratio Drift Across Batch Exports** | Low / Medium (Sub-HD softness or cropping on mobile) | `engine/asset_audit.py` automatically verifies `9:16` aspect ratio, `>=720x1280` resolution, `<=60s` duration, and H.264/AAC streams on all 10 clean Shorts (`YT-03` was caught at `360x640` and upscaled via Lanczos to `720x1280`, bringing automated pass rate to `30/30`). |
| **4. Low Early Organic Reach on Cold Brand Accounts** | High / Medium (Slow Week 1–2 impressions) | Pair high-save carousel formats (`LI-01..07`, `IG-01..08`) with discovery-driven YouTube Shorts (`YT-01..10`), engage manually in creator comments for 15 mins/day, and optionally allocate `$150–$300` in Weeks 3–4 only to the single highest-converting organic post. |
| **5. LLM Provider Rate-Limit or Transient API Failure During Weekly Run** | Medium / Low (Delays Monday batch generation) | `engine/pipeline.py` includes automatic retry/recovery (`max_attempts_per_platform: 2`), structured JSONL error logging (`pipeline.log`), and deterministic fallback so one platform failure never corrupts the ledger or workbook. |
