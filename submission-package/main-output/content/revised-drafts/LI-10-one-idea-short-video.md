# LI-10 — Turn One Useful Idea into a Short Video
Status: EDUCATIONAL DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION
Platform: LinkedIn
Format: Short video script
Target audience: Creators, podcasters, YouTubers, editors, and small content teams
Product-specific claims: None

## Video concept
Explain one practical lesson in a focused, accessible short video.

## Script and scene plan
Target duration: approximately 35–45 seconds.

0–5 sec — Hook
On-screen text: "One useful idea. One focused video."
Voiceover: "Before recording, decide what you want viewers to remember."

5–15 sec — Define the idea
Voiceover: "Choose one question your audience cares about, then state the main lesson in one sentence."

15–28 sec — Explain it
Voiceover: "Give one practical example or demonstrate one step. Leave unrelated ideas for another post."

28–38 sec — Summarise
Voiceover: "Repeat the takeaway so viewers can remember the point."

38–45 sec — Closing
On-screen text: "What is one lesson you could explain today?"
Voiceover: "Start with one useful idea and build from there."

## Caption
A focused short video gives the viewer one clear takeaway. Keep the hook honest, the explanation useful, and the text readable.

## Hashtags

## CTA
Choose one question and write its answer in a single sentence before recording.

## Production notes
Use original footage or authorised assets. Add accurate captions and check that the video remains understandable without sound.

## Evidence and rights
Illustrative educational script. No promise of reach or engagement.

## Production checklist
Record, caption, check audio and rights, review on mobile, and verify the final duration is under 60 seconds.
