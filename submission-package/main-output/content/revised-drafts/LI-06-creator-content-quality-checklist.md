## Main message
A final content review should check more than spelling.

## Graphic copy
BEFORE YOU PUBLISH

1. Are the facts supported?
2. Does the hook match the content?
3. Are captions and text readable?
4. Are images, clips, and music authorised?
5. Does the CTA point to a real next step?
6. Has the content received the required review?

## Caption
Whether you publish videos, podcast clips, or educational posts, a short review can help you catch missing information before it goes live. Treat accuracy, accessibility, permissions, links, and approval as separate checks.
