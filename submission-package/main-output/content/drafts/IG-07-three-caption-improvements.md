# IG-07 — Three Ways to Improve a Caption
Status: EDUCATIONAL DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION

Format: Short video script
Product-specific claims: None

## Video concept
A quick educational video showing three practical caption edits.

## Script and scene plan
Target duration: 30–40 seconds.

0–4 sec — Hook, on-screen text: "Make your next caption clearer."
Voiceover: "Try these three simple edits."

4–14 sec — Edit one: specificity.
Voiceover: "Replace a broad opening with a clear audience situation."

14–24 sec — Edit two: readability.
Voiceover: "Break long blocks into short, easy-to-scan sentences."

24–33 sec — Edit three: one action.
Voiceover: "Give the reader one relevant next step instead of several competing requests."

33–40 sec — Closing.
Voiceover: "Clear, useful, and focused. Start with that."

## Caption
A useful caption sets context, makes the message easy to read, and gives the audience one clear next step.
