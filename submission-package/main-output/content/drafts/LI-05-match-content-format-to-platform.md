# LI-05 - Match Content Format to Platform

Status: EDUCATIONAL DRAFT - NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION
Platform: LinkedIn

## Slide 1 - Cover
Choose the format that fits the message.

## Slide 2 - Start with the purpose
Decide whether the content should explain, teach, demonstrate, or start a conversation.

## Slide 3 - Use carousels for structured ideas
A carousel can organize a process, checklist, or sequence into manageable steps.

## Slide 4 - Use short videos for demonstrations
Short videos can show a process or explain an idea visually. Keep the message focused and captions readable.

## Slide 5 - Use graphics for one key takeaway
A graphic can highlight one main point, comparison, or reminder without overcrowding the design.

## Slide 6 - Adapt instead of copying
Adjust the opening, length, layout, and call to action to fit the platform and the audience. Check current platform requirements before publishing.

## Caption
One idea can take different forms, but the format should serve the message.

Use a carousel to organize steps, a short video to demonstrate a process, or a graphic to emphasize one key takeaway. These are starting points, not guaranteed performance outcomes.

Choose the format that makes the information easiest for your intended audience to understand.
