## Slide 1 — Cover
Five Steps to Plan a Content Week

## Slide 2 — Pick one goal
Decide what this week's content should help the audience understand or do.

## Slide 3 — Choose an audience question
Start with a real question, challenge, or learning need.

## Slide 4 — Build a small content mix
Plan a useful explanation, a practical example, and an actionable checklist.

## Slide 5 — Assign publishing dates
Give every planned post a date and a clear purpose.

## Slide 6 — Review before sharing
Check accuracy, readability, rights, links, and the call to action.

## Caption
A content week is easier to manage when every post has a purpose. Start with one goal, choose a real audience question, and plan a manageable mix of useful posts.
