## Main message
A quick quality check helps you catch avoidable content issues before sharing.

## Checklist

1. Purpose: Is the main message clear?
2. Audience: Is the content useful for its intended audience?
3. Accuracy: Can factual statements be supported by reliable evidence?
4. Clarity: Is the wording simple, readable, and specific?
5. Brand: Does the content follow the approved voice and style?
6. Visuals: Are text, spacing, contrast, and layout readable?
7. Rights: Do you have permission to use every image, clip, and other asset?
8. Links: Do links and calls to action lead to the intended destination?
9. Platform: Have current format and platform requirements been checked?
10. Approval: Has the required human review been completed?

## Caption
Before you share a post, pause for a quick quality check.

Review the purpose, audience, evidence, readability, visual design, usage rights, links, platform requirements, and approval status.

A checklist cannot guarantee results, but it can make your review process more consistent.

Save this list and use it during your next content review.
