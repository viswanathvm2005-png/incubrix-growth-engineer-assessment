## Slide 1 - Cover
One useful source can inspire multiple content ideas.

## Slide 2 - Start with a source
Choose an article, presentation, webinar, report, or other source you have permission to use.

## Slide 3 - Find the central message
Summarize the main idea in one sentence. Keep the original meaning intact.

## Slide 4 - Extract smaller ideas
Identify useful steps, questions, examples, common mistakes, and practical takeaways.

## Slide 5 - Adapt the ideas
Turn suitable ideas into a checklist, carousel, short video, graphic, or discussion prompt. Rewrite each version for its intended audience and platform.

## Slide 6 - Verify before sharing
Check facts against the original source, confirm usage rights, review links, and make sure every version is useful on its own.

## Caption
You do not always need to start from a blank page.

Begin with a useful source you have permission to use. Identify its main message, extract smaller insights, and consider which formats could make those insights easier to understand.

Repurposing is not simply copying the same text everywhere. Adapt each version to its audience, verify the facts, and preserve the original meaning.
