# YT-10 — Write a Clear Call to Action

**Platform:** YouTube Shorts
**Asset type:** Short video script
**Target duration:** 30–45 seconds; final edit must be 60 seconds or less
**Status:** Draft — NOT approved or published
**Audience:** Creators, editors, and small content teams
**Content category:** Educational creator workflow

## 1. Opening hook
Give viewers one clear next step.

## 2. Voiceover script
A call to action should tell viewers what to do and why it is relevant. Instead of asking people to like, comment, subscribe, share, and click all at once, choose one action that fits the video. For a practical checklist, ask viewers to save it. Make the destination clear whenever you ask them to click.

## 3. Visual and editing instructions
Show a crowded list of actions, then simplify it to one relevant action.

Use original visuals or assets with documented usage permission. Keep text readable on a mobile screen. Use accurate captions and review the final cut for context and accessibility.

## 4. On-screen text
ONE VIDEO
ONE PRIMARY ACTION

## 5. YouTube description / caption
Choose a call to action that matches the value of your video. Only include a link when the destination is confirmed and available.
