# YT-06 — Repurpose a Long Video

**Platform:** YouTube Shorts
**Asset type:** Short video script
**Target duration:** 30–45 seconds; final edit must be 60 seconds or less
**Status:** Draft — NOT approved or published
**Audience:** Creators, editors, and small content teams
**Content category:** Educational creator workflow

## 1. Opening hook
Have a long video sitting unused?

## 2. Voiceover script
Review the full video and identify moments that each teach one complete idea. Select a section with enough context to stand alone. Rewrite the opening for short-form viewing, remove unnecessary pauses, add accurate captions, and check the final edit against the original. Do not take a clip out of context.

## 3. Visual and editing instructions
Show a long timeline, select one self-contained segment, then add captions and a new opening.

Use original visuals or assets with documented usage permission. Keep text readable on a mobile screen. Use accurate captions and review the final cut for context and accessibility.

## 4. On-screen text
FIND ONE IDEA
KEEP THE CONTEXT
EDIT FOR SHORTS

## 5. YouTube description / caption
A good short clip should make sense on its own and preserve the original meaning. Check context, captions, and rights before publishing.
