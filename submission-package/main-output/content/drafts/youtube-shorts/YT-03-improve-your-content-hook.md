# YT-03 — Improve Your Content Hook

**Platform:** YouTube Shorts
**Asset type:** Short video script
**Target duration:** 30–45 seconds; final edit must be 60 seconds or less
**Status:** Draft — NOT approved or published
**Audience:** Creators, editors, and small content teams
**Content category:** Educational creator workflow

## 1. Opening hook
Would you keep watching after this opening?

## 2. Voiceover script
A strong hook makes the topic and value clear quickly. Instead of saying, 'Here are some content tips,' try, 'Three checks to make before publishing your next post.' The second opening tells viewers what they will get. Be specific, stay honest, and make sure the video delivers on its promise.

## 3. Visual and editing instructions
Compare a vague opening with a specific opening. Highlight the promised value.

Use original visuals or assets with documented usage permission. Keep text readable on a mobile screen. Use accurate captions and review the final cut for context and accessibility.

## 4. On-screen text
BE SPECIFIC
SHOW THE VALUE
DELIVER THE PROMISE

## 5. YouTube description / caption
Make your opening specific enough that viewers understand what they will learn. Never promise something the video does not deliver.
