## 2. What this evidence does not prove

- That any feature was tested by this candidate.
- That a feature works for every account or platform.
- That any feature saves a specific amount of time.
- That any feature improves reach, engagement, or sign-ups.
- That any particular platform integration is currently operational.
- That the candidate can access or demonstrate these features.
- That website examples or illustrative numbers are real customer results.

## 3. Approved wording for draft planning

Use qualified wording such as:
- "The IncuBrix website describes..."
- "The public website presents this as..."
- "The website labels this capability as coming soon."
- "This workflow needs confirmation through product access."

Do not turn website copy into a personal product observation.
Do not invent product screenshots, customer testimonials, or test results.

## 4. Required next evidence

- Current source URL and date checked.

- Test steps and observed result, if actually tested.
- Limitations or unresolved questions.
- Review decision and approved wording.
