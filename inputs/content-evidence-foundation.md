# Content Evidence Foundation

Status: PLANNING ONLY — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION
Prepared: 2026-10-19

## 1. Purpose

Define which content can be drafted from currently available evidence and
which content must wait for direct product evidence or candidate validation.

This document does not authorize publication or imply that any account is
officially controlled by the candidate.

## 2. Evidence available

- The supplied IncuBrix assessment brief.
- A preliminary review of public pages recorded in
  `public-product-claims-register.md`.
- The public claims register identifies claims as unverified by hands-on testing.

## 3. Content categories

### Category A — Educational content that can be drafted

These topics can be developed as general educational material without
claiming that IncuBrix provides a particular feature or achieves a particular result.

- Planning a repeatable social media content workflow.
- Turning one long-form source into platform-specific content formats.
- Writing stronger hooks and clear calls to action.
- Building a practical content calendar.
- Creating captions and adapting tone for different platforms.
- Checking drafts for unsupported claims and rights issues.
- Tracking content performance without inventing results.
- Reviewing content and recording lessons from real audience feedback.

All examples must be labelled as educational examples where appropriate.
Do not present hypothetical metrics as actual results.

### Category B — Product-related drafts requiring careful qualification

Public-page claims may inform draft concepts about content insights,
content creation, editing, captions, narration, avatars, and publishing.

Before using a product feature as a factual claim, verify the relevant page,
record the access date, preserve supporting evidence, and distinguish a
publicly advertised feature from a feature personally tested.

Do not imply hands-on experience that did not occur.

### Category C — Blocked pending evidence

- Five genuine observations from direct product use.
- Verified product walkthrough screenshots or recordings.
- Confirmation of feature availability and platform integrations.
- Any claim about product accuracy, speed, reliability, or results.
- Any claim about customer outcomes, reach, engagement, or sign-ups.
- Competitor comparisons based on current, comparable evidence.
- Audience-test findings before a real test is conducted.

For each missing item, record:
UNKNOWN - needs input from candidate

## 4. Rules for all future assets

1. No fabricated product capabilities, testimonials, metrics, or case studies.
2. No unsupported comparative or performance claims.
3. No use of third-party images, music, clips, or other protected material
   without a suitable rights basis.
4. No publication or scheduling to live accounts during this assessment workflow.
5. No contact with creators or brands on behalf of IncuBrix.
6. Every asset must have a traceable source and an editable source file.
7. Label mock content and hypothetical examples clearly.
8. Do not mark an asset ready for publication until the required evidence,
   rights, brand, and human-review checks are satisfied.
