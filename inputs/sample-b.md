# Turning one weekly podcast episode into a week of posts

Many small creator teams record one long episode each week and then struggle to decide what to publish from it. A workable approach starts with the audience, not the calendar. Pick one question that listeners have actually asked, then find the two or three moments in the episode that answer it clearly.

Each moment can become a different format. A short clip works for a single practical tip. A carousel suits a sequence of steps, such as a checklist of what to prepare before recording. A text post can explain the reasoning behind a decision the guest made.

Before anything is scheduled, check the basics: the quote is accurate and in context, the guest has agreed to the clip being shared, any music or images are licensed, and the call to action points to a page that exists. Keep a simple log of which episode each post came from so a reviewer can trace every claim back to the recording.

Finally, record what happened. Note actual views, saves and clicks only when the platform reports them, and use those real numbers to choose next week's question. Avoid promising results to the audience; teach one useful thing per post instead.
