## 1. Strategic Objective

Build a repeatable, low-lift organic social growth engine across **LinkedIn**, **Instagram**, and **YouTube Shorts** that:
1. Teaches creators and lean content teams how to turn **one idea or long-form source into a full week of platform-native content** without generic AI slop.
2. Demonstrates IncuBrix's guided ideation and script workflow (`AI Script Assistant`: `Format -> Structure -> Treatment -> Personal Angle -> Multi-Angle Cards`) using only verified product evidence.
3. Converts engaged viewers into free-tier workspace sign-ups (`https://app.incubrix.com/`, `300 credits` starting balance) through measurable UTM-tracked calls to action.

## 2. Target Audience & Geographies

### 2.1 Primary Audience — Independent Creators
- **Segments**:
  - **YouTubers & Video Podcasters**: Record 20–90 minute episodes weekly; need to extract short-form vertical hooks (`<=60s`) and written posts without spending 10+ hours re-editing and rewriting.
  - **Expert-Led Creators & Educator-Founders**: Consultants, technical educators, and niche analysts who publish insights on LinkedIn and YouTube/Instagram and care deeply about factual accuracy, voice integrity, and non-generic hooks.
- **Core Pain Points**:
  1. **Blank-page & daily decision fatigue**: Waking up every morning wondering what angle to post.
  2. **Cross-platform adaptation friction**: Copy-pasting the same caption across LinkedIn, Instagram, and Shorts fails because each platform rewards different native structures.
  3. **Distrust of generic AI output**: One-shot prompts produce repetitive clichés and fabricated claims that damage creator credibility.

### 2.2 Secondary Audience — The People Who Help Them
- **Segments**:
  - **Freelance Video/Content Editors** and **Small Content Teams (1–3 people)** supporting creators or founder-led brands.
- **Core Pain Points**:
  1. Turning a client's raw recording or rough note into an approved 7-day publishing calendar quickly.
  2. Running consistent pre-publish quality checks (hooks, captions, visual dimensions, usage rights, AI disclosures, and links) before client sign-off.

### 2.3 Priority Geographies (Per Official Specification)
- **Primary English-Speaking Creator Markets**:
  - **North America**: United States (`US`), Canada (`CA`)
  - **UK & Ireland**: United Kingdom (`UK`), Ireland (`IE`)
  - **Asia-Pacific**: Australia (`AU`), New Zealand (`NZ`), Singapore (`SG`)
- **Scheduling Implication**: Default publishing windows are set to **09:00–11:00 US Eastern (`13:00–15:00 UTC`)** for LinkedIn/Instagram (capturing US/CA morning + UK/IE afternoon) and **12:00–15:00 US Eastern (`16:00–19:00 UTC`)** for YouTube Shorts.

## 5. Brand Voice Rules & Factual Claim Governance

Enforced programmatically via `config/brand-voice.yaml`, `config/authorized-Source-and-Claim-Policy.yaml`, `engine/factual_language_review.py`, and `engine/asset_audit.py`:

1. **Voice Attributes**:
   - **Practical & Specific**: Every post must teach a concrete workflow step, checklist, or script structure in the first slide/sentence.
   - **Calm & Credible**: Plain English; short sentences; zero jargon where a simple word works.
   - **Creator-Respectful**: Treat AI as a structured assistant to human judgment (`Ready to draft` vs `Needs one detail`), never a replacement for creator expertise.
2. **Prohibited Language & Claim Categories (`0` Tolerance)**:
   - **No Guaranteed Outcomes or Unverified Multipliers**: Never claim `"10x faster"`, `"guaranteed viral reach"`, `"double your followers"`, or `"proven ROI"`. (Disclaimers such as `"not guaranteed performance outcomes"` in `LI-05` and `"cannot guarantee results"` in `LI-06` are explicitly permitted.)
   - **No Fabricated Social Proof**: Never invent customer names, creator testimonials, case-study percentages, or benchmark wins.
   - **No Unverified Product Claims**: Only reference product behaviors directly verified in `inputs/product-evidence-log.md` (`AI Script Assistant` 4-step guided intake and angle cards). Never advertise `"Coming soon"` items (`Content Performance`, `Collaborators`, `Comments and Discussions`) as live features.
   - **Rights & AI Disclosure Integrity**: Keep `rights_checked = No` until every font, visual, and audio track is human-cleared in `launch-kit/rights-inventory.csv`. Apply platform AI disclosures (`ai_label_needed = Yes`) wherever synthetic visuals or voices are used (specifically `YT-01`..`YT-10`).

## 6. Weekly Posting Frequency & Sustainable 30-Day Cadence

To publish all **30 ready assets across 30 days** (and sustain weekly publishing thereafter in `<30 minutes` of engine generation + human review):

- **Weekly Cadence (7–8 posts/week)**:
  - **LinkedIn**: **2–3 posts/week** (Tuesday, Thursday, and alternating Saturday mornings — Carousels, Checklist Graphic, and Text frameworks).
  - **Instagram**: **2–3 posts/week** (Monday, Wednesday, and alternating Friday mornings — 4:5 Educational Carousels and Checklist Graphics).
  - **YouTube Shorts**: **2–3 Shorts/week** (Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday afternoons — `<=24s` 9:16 vertical videos).
- **Why This Cadence Works**:
  - One weekly long-form input (a blog post, product changelog note, or webinar transcript) run through `py -3.12 -m engine pipeline --input <file> --source-id <id>` generates **12 platform-specific drafts (`4 LinkedIn, 4 Instagram, 4 YouTube Shorts`)** in a single batch.
  - Selecting the top 7–8 drafts after human review fills an entire week of publishing with a 4-asset buffer.

## 7. How Social Activity Turns Into Sign-Ups (Conversion Path)

```
[1. Platform-Native Hook (First Line / First 2s)]
   Addresses a specific creator bottleneck (e.g., blank-page morning, 1-to-many repurposing, weak hooks)
        │
        ▼
[2. Standalone Educational Value (Carousel / Short Video / Checklist)]
   Proves the method works manually (e.g., 4-step angle constraint, 5-point pre-publish check)
        │
        ▼
[3. Single Actionable CTA + Tracked UTM Link]
   Low-friction micro-action (save/comment) + profile/description UTM link:
   ?utm_source={linkedin|instagram|youtube_shorts}&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=incubrix_content_engine&utm_content={asset_id}
   (Held as UNKNOWN in calendar.csv until candidate approves destination URL)
        │
        ▼
[4. Free-Tier Workspace Landing (`https://app.incubrix.com/` — 300 Credits)]
   Visitor signs up for free (300 credits, no paid barrier required to test)
        │
        ▼
[5. First-Session Activation (`AI Script Assistant` Guided Intake)]
   New user enters 1 topic -> completes 4-step guided intake (`Format -> Structure -> Treatment -> Personal Angle`)
   -> receives 3 tailored angle cards (`Playbook`, `Observation`, `Prediction`) in under 2 minutes.
```
