## 4. Required record for every asset

Record the following fields in the asset tracker:
- Asset ID
- Platform
- Format
- Topic and audience hypothesis
- Hook
- Caption
- Hashtags
- CTA
- Tracking URL
- Evidence/source reference
- Editable source-file path
- Rights/permission status
- Factual review status
- Human review status
- Final readiness status

Use UNKNOWN - needs input from candidate for missing facts.
Do not invent URLs, product capabilities, audience findings, or results.

## 5. Evidence and safety gates

An asset cannot be marked ready unless:
1. Every product-specific claim has an approved evidence source.
2. Any demonstration accurately reflects the real product.
3. Copyright, permissions, and asset provenance are checked.
4. Platform-specific rules and format limits are checked.
5. CTA destinations and tracking links are verified.
6. Required human review is complete.

Do not publish, contact creators or brands, or represent this project
as an official IncuBrix account without authorization.

- Dated audit of five relevant competitors or peers.

- Original creative assets and editable source files.
- Required real-audience test or preference test with at least 20 people.
