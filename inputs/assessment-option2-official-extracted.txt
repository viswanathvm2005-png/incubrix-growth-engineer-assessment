6. How we check your work
• IncuBrix recalculates every number from your files. Totals typed into the workbook are not official.
• We pick 20 assets at random from your main output (you cannot choose or replace them) and check each one against the 
quality bar.
• We run your engine ourselves, using only your README.
• Live review (45 minutes): you trace 5 random assets back to their evidence, run your engine on a small new input we give 
you, and explain one decision you would change.
• A growth reviewer and an engineering reviewer score separately. Any change to a score must point to evidence.
7. Exceptional result
• 60 ready assets, including at least 20 videos, with at least 90% passing our audit.
• The real-audience test beats a stated, sourced benchmark for engagement or preference.
• The engine produces a full week (at least 20 assets) from one input in under 30 minutes, with cost documented.
• A creator-collaboration or user-generated content format that is ready to run.
8. Tools and integrity rules
• Use any AI tool, data source or service, free or paid. List them all in the workbook. No paid tool is required, and IncuBrix 
does not refund costs unless agreed in writing first.
• Work on your own. Disclose any manual work and where AI helped.
• Publish only on accounts you own, and never present them as official IncuBrix accounts. Do not contact creators or 
brands. Do not buy followers or engagement.
• No fake, bought or guessed data, contacts, followers, engagement or results. Do not scrape against a site's terms, bypass 
logins or use private or leaked data.
• Follow privacy and marketing laws (for example India's DPDP Act, GDPR and CAN-SPAM) and every platform's rules.
