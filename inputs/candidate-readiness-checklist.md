# IncuBrix Candidate-Input Readiness Checklist

Purpose: Identify candidate-provided information needed before creating real IncuBrix marketing content.

Rule: Do not invent product facts, customer results, metrics, pricing, integrations, guarantees, or competitor comparisons.

Use these statuses:
- UNKNOWN - needs input from candidate
- PROVIDED - candidate supplied the information
- VERIFIED - supporting evidence has been checked
- NOT APPLICABLE - genuinely does not apply; explain why

## Release rule

Do not treat this checklist as proof that a product claim is true.
Do not mark a claim VERIFIED without checking its supporting evidence.
Do not mark an asset ready or publish it merely because this checklist exists.
Keep missing facts explicitly unknown until the candidate supplies them.
No publishing or external contact is authorized by this document.
