# YT-07 — Make Captions Clearer

**Platform:** YouTube Shorts
**Asset type:** Short video script
**Target duration:** 30–45 seconds; final edit must be 60 seconds or less
**Status:** Draft — NOT approved or published
**Audience:** Creators, editors, and small content teams
**Content category:** Educational creator workflow

## 1. Opening hook
Your caption may need less, not more.

## 2. Voiceover script
Try three simple edits. Put the main point first. Replace vague words with concrete details you can support. Remove sentences that repeat the same idea. Then give readers one clear next step. Keep the caption useful on its own, and do not add facts just to make it sound impressive.

## 3. Visual and editing instructions
Edit a deliberately vague sample caption into a concise, clearer version.

Use original visuals or assets with documented usage permission. Keep text readable on a mobile screen. Use accurate captions and review the final cut for context and accessibility.

## 4. On-screen text
MAIN POINT FIRST
REMOVE REPETITION
ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

## 5. YouTube description / caption
Clear captions help people understand the point without searching through unnecessary words. Keep every factual detail supportable.
