# YT-01 — Plan a Content Week

**Platform:** YouTube Shorts
**Asset type:** Short video script
**Target duration:** 30–45 seconds; final edit must be 60 seconds or less
**Status:** Draft — NOT approved or published
**Audience:** Creators, editors, and small content teams
**Content category:** Educational creator workflow

## 1. Opening hook
Still deciding what to post every morning?

## 2. Voiceover script
Stop planning your content one post at a time. First, choose one audience problem. Next, turn it into three useful ideas. Assign each idea a format and a publishing day. Finally, prepare your captions and visuals together. A simple weekly plan can make your next steps clearer.

## 3. Visual and editing instructions
Open on a blank calendar. Add one audience problem, three idea cards, and a sample weekly schedule.

Use original visuals or assets with documented usage permission. Keep text readable on a mobile screen. Use accurate captions and review the final cut for context and accessibility.
