# YT-05 — Pre-Publish Quality Check

**Platform:** YouTube Shorts
**Asset type:** Short video script
**Target duration:** 30–45 seconds; final edit must be 60 seconds or less
**Status:** Draft — NOT approved or published
**Audience:** Creators, editors, and small content teams
**Content category:** Educational creator workflow

## 1. Opening hook
Before you publish, run these five checks.

## 2. Voiceover script
Check the facts. Check spelling and readability. Confirm you have permission to use every visual and audio asset. Make sure the call to action works. Finally, preview the post in its intended format. If a claim or permission is uncertain, pause and verify it instead of guessing.

## 3. Visual and editing instructions
Show five checklist items, with uncertain facts and rights marked for review.

Use original visuals or assets with documented usage permission. Keep text readable on a mobile screen. Use accurate captions and review the final cut for context and accessibility.

## 10. Production and approval checklist
- [ ] Record or create visuals using permitted assets.
- [ ] Confirm the finished video is 60 seconds or less.
- [ ] Check spelling, captions, audio, and mobile readability.
- [ ] Verify all factual statements.
- [ ] Confirm visual/audio rights and any applicable disclosure requirements.
- [ ] Add a verified CTA destination and tracking URL, or document why they are not applicable.
- [ ] Human review completed.
- [ ] Final video exported and checked.
- [ ] Approval recorded before any publication.
