## Slide 1 — Cover
Posting feels chaotic? Build a repeatable content workflow.

## Slide 2 — Start with one goal
Decide what the content should help your audience learn, understand, or do.

## Slide 3 — Choose one audience problem
Focus on a specific question instead of trying to cover everything.

## Slide 4 — Create the core idea
Write the main message in a few sentences before choosing a format.

## Slide 5 — Adapt for each platform
Adjust the opening, length, structure, and call to action to suit the platform.

## Slide 6 — Review before sharing
Check accuracy, readability, rights, links, and whether the content delivers on its opening promise.

## Caption
A repeatable content workflow starts before the first post is written.

Define the goal, choose one audience problem, develop the core idea, adapt it for the platform, and review it before sharing.
