## Slide 1 — Cover
One useful idea. Multiple content formats.

## Slide 2 — Find the central message
Choose one long-form source you have the right to use. Identify its main takeaway.

## Slide 3 — Extract useful parts
Look for key questions, practical steps, common mistakes, examples, and useful ideas.

## Slide 4 — Match format to idea
A checklist may suit a graphic. A step-by-step explanation may suit a carousel. A concise demonstration may suit a short video.

## Slide 5 — Adapt each version
Rewrite the hook and supporting copy for the format and audience. Do not assume every platform needs identical wording.

## Slide 6 — Check before sharing
Verify the source, factual claims, permissions, links, and final creative files.

## Caption
Repurposing is not just copying the same post several times.

Start with a source you are allowed to use, identify its strongest ideas, and reshape each idea for the format and audience.

Which format would you try first: a carousel, a graphic, or a short video?
