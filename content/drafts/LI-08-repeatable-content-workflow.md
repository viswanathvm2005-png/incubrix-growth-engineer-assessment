## Slide 1 - Cover
A repeatable workflow makes content creation easier to manage.

## Slide 2 - Start with a goal
Decide what the content should help the audience understand, consider, or do.

## Slide 3 - Gather reliable input
Start with a source you can access and have permission to use. Record the important facts.

## Slide 4 - Create and adapt
Develop the main idea, then adapt the wording and format to the intended audience and platform.

## Slide 5 - Run the quality checks
Check accuracy, readability, brand voice, visual clarity, usage rights, links, and platform requirements.

## Slide 6 - Review and organize
Save editable sources, record asset details, complete human review, and keep approval status clear.

## Caption
Good content creation is more than writing a caption and choosing a visual.

A repeatable workflow helps you move from a clear goal to reliable source material, useful content, quality checks, and organized final assets.

Keep the steps simple enough to repeat. Document what needs review, who is responsible, and what must be completed before an asset is approved.
