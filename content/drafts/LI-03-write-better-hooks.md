## Slide 1 — Cover
Your opening line has one job: give people a reason to continue.

## Slide 2 — Be specific
Instead of "Some content tips," try "Three checks to make before sharing a post."

## Slide 3 — Lead with the problem
Name a real question or difficulty your intended audience may recognize.

## Slide 4 — Make the value clear
Tell readers what they can learn, compare, or complete by continuing.

## Slide 5 — Keep the promise
The content must deliver what the opening suggests. Avoid exaggerated claims.

## Slide 6 — Try this quick test
Can a reader understand the topic, intended audience, and value from the opening alone?

If not, revise it.

## Caption
A strong hook does not need to exaggerate.

Try making the topic specific, naming the problem, and explaining what the reader will get from the content. Then make sure the rest of the post delivers on that promise.
