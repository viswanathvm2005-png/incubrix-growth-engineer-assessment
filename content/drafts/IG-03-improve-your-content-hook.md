## Slide 1 — Cover
Make Your First Line More Specific

## Slide 2 — Before
"Here are some content tips."

## Slide 3 — What is missing?
The topic is broad. The reader does not know what they will learn.

## Slide 4 — After
"Planning next week's posts? Start with these three questions."

## Slide 5 — Why it is clearer
It identifies a situation and tells the reader what to expect.

## Slide 6 — Try it yourself
Name the audience situation, then preview one useful takeaway.

## Caption
A strong opening helps people understand why a post may be relevant. Specificity is more useful than promising an outcome you cannot support.
