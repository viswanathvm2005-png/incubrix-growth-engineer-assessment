## Video concept
Demonstrate a repeatable way to turn one permitted long-form source into a focused short video.

## Script and scene plan
Target duration: 35–45 seconds.

0–5 sec — Hook, on-screen text: "One long source. One focused short video."
Voiceover: "You don't need to squeeze an entire presentation into one short video."

5–15 sec — Select a point.
Voiceover: "Choose one useful lesson from a source you have permission to reuse."

15–27 sec — Explain the lesson.
Voiceover: "State the problem, explain the key idea, and give one practical example."

27–37 sec — Finish clearly.
Voiceover: "End with one action the viewer can try."

37–45 sec — Closing text.
Voiceover: "Keep the lesson focused, and credit your source when appropriate."

## Caption
Repurposing works best when each short video delivers one complete takeaway. Select a single lesson instead of trying to summarise everything at once.
