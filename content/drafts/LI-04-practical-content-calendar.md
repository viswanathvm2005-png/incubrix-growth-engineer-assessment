## Slide 1 — Cover
A practical content calendar starts with a clear goal.

## Slide 2 — Choose one goal
Decide what this planning period should support: awareness, education, engagement, or sign-ups.

## Slide 3 — Define your audience
Write down who the content is for and what question or problem it should address.

## Slide 4 — Plan content pillars
Choose a small set of recurring themes. Each theme should support the goal and provide useful information.

## Slide 5 — Assign dates and formats
Give each idea a planned date, platform, format, owner, and review status. Leave room for timely updates.

## Slide 6 — Review before scheduling
Check the facts, links, rights, platform requirements, and approval status before content is scheduled.

## Caption
A content calendar is more than a list of dates.

Start with one goal, identify the audience, group ideas into useful themes, and assign each item a format and review status. Keep the plan flexible enough to accommodate relevant updates.

Use this checklist to make your next planning session more structured.
