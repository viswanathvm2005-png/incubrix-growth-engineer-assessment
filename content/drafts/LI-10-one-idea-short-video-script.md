# LI-10 - Turn One Idea into a Short Video

Status: EDUCATIONAL DRAFT - NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION
Platform: LinkedIn
Format: Short video script
Target duration: Approximately 30-40 seconds
Product-specific claims: None

## Video concept
Show how to turn one clear idea into a short, useful educational video.

## Script and scene plan

### Scene 1 - Hook (0-4 seconds)
On-screen text: One idea is enough to start.
Voiceover: "Struggling to decide what to post? Start with just one useful idea."

### Scene 2 - Choose the idea (4-10 seconds)
On-screen text: Step 1 - Pick one takeaway
Voiceover: "Choose one question, lesson, or practical tip that your audience may find useful."

### Scene 3 - Build the message (10-18 seconds)
On-screen text: Step 2 - Keep it simple
Voiceover: "Explain the idea in a few clear sentences. Remove details that distract from the main point."

### Scene 4 - Add a visual (18-26 seconds)
On-screen text: Step 3 - Show, don't just tell
Voiceover: "Use a simple example, a short checklist, or a visual that helps people understand the message."

### Scene 5 - Review (26-33 seconds)
On-screen text: Step 4 - Check before sharing
Voiceover: "Check your facts, readability, visual rights, and whether the video delivers on its promise."

### Scene 6 - CTA (33-38 seconds)
On-screen text: What is your one useful idea?
Voiceover: "Choose one idea today and turn it into your next short video."

## Caption
You do not need to explain everything in one post.

Start with one useful idea, build a clear message around it, and choose a visual that supports the explanation. Before sharing, check accuracy, readability, and usage rights.

A focused video is easier to plan when you know the one thing you want the audience to take away.
