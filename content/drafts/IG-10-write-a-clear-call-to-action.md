## Main message
Make the next step specific and relevant.

## Graphic copy
WRITE A CLEAR CTA

1. What should the reader do?
2. Why is it relevant to this post?
3. Is the next step available and accurate?

Example:
Broad: "Learn more."
Specific: "Save this checklist for your next planning session."

## Caption
A call to action should match the content and the next step you can genuinely offer. Avoid implying a link, service, or resource exists unless it does.
