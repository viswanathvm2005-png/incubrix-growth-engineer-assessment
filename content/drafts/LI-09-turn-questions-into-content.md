## Slide 1 - Cover
One good question can inspire a useful content idea.

## Slide 2 - Listen for questions
Notice the questions your intended audience asks repeatedly. Use feedback and conversations you are permitted to reference.

## Slide 3 - Group related questions
Organize questions into themes such as getting started, common mistakes, practical steps, and decision-making.

## Slide 4 - Choose one question
Select a question that is relevant, specific, and useful to answer in a single piece of content.

## Slide 5 - Pick the right format
Explain a process with a checklist, compare options with a simple graphic, or demonstrate a task in a short video.

## Slide 6 - Answer with care
Check the facts, protect private information, avoid assumptions, and give the audience a clear next step.

## Caption
Not every content idea needs to start with brainstorming.

Questions from your intended audience can reveal topics worth explaining. Look for recurring themes, choose one focused question, and answer it in a format that makes the information easy to understand.

Always protect private information and verify your answer before sharing it.
