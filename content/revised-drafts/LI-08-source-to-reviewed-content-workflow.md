## Post copy
When several people work on content, the handoffs can be as important as the creative idea.

A simple workflow can make those handoffs visible:

Source → Brief → Draft → Platform adaptation → Rights and fact check → Human review → Approved asset

For every item, record the source, owner, status, and next action. Keep drafts separate from approved assets so that a planned post is never mistaken for a final one.

The best workflow is one your team can actually maintain.

## Caption
Creators, editors, and small marketing teams can start by making the next step and responsible owner visible. Add complexity only when it solves a real coordination problem.
