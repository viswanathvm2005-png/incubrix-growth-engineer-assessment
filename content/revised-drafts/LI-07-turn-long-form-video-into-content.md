## Post copy
A long-form video may contain several lessons. The useful starting point is not to cut it into random pieces; it is to identify what each piece helps the audience understand.

Try this process:

1. Review the source and note its strongest self-contained ideas.
2. Choose one idea that makes sense without misleading context.
3. Adapt that idea into a short clip, a written explanation, or a checklist.
4. Keep the original source recorded for attribution and review.
5. Check permissions, captions, and accuracy before publishing.

Each derivative should deliver a complete takeaway while staying faithful to the source.
