## Post copy
Running out of content ideas? Start with the questions your audience already asks.

Try this four-step exercise:

1. Review comments, messages, interviews, or questions you are permitted to use.
2. Group similar questions into themes.
3. Choose one question you can answer accurately.
4. Create a post that answers it directly, with an example or practical next step.

Remove private details and ask permission where needed. Do not treat a few questions as proof that every audience member has the same need.

## Caption
Audience questions can provide a useful starting point for relevant content. Handle feedback responsibly and make sure the finished post answers the question rather than merely using it as a hook.
