## Slide 1 — Cover
One Podcast Episode. Multiple Content Ideas.

## Slide 2 — Find the strongest lesson
Choose one clear insight from an episode you own or have permission to reuse.

## Slide 3 — Create a short clip
Select a self-contained excerpt and check that it makes sense without the full episode.

## Slide 4 — Write a text post
Explain the insight in your own words and add context for readers who have not listened.

## Slide 5 — Build a carousel
Break the lesson into a few practical steps or questions.

## Slide 6 — Keep the source connected
Record the original episode and check permissions, attribution, and context for every derivative.

## Caption
A podcast episode can contain several useful lessons, but each derivative should stand on its own. Start with one insight, adapt it for the format, and preserve the original meaning.
