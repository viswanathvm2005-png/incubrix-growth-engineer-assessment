## Slide 1 — Cover
A Realistic Content Calendar for a Small Team

## Slide 2 — Start with capacity
Choose a posting cadence your team can produce and review consistently.

## Slide 3 — Give each post a purpose
Record the audience need and the main takeaway.

## Slide 4 — Add production details
Include platform, format, owner, source, draft location, and due date.

## Slide 5 — Make review visible
Track whether the content is planned, drafted, under review, approved, or published.

## Slide 6 — Leave room to adapt
Review the calendar regularly and adjust based on actual capacity and evidence.

## Caption
A calendar should make work clearer, not create an unrealistic publishing promise. For small creator teams, visible ownership, review status, and production capacity matter as much as dates.
