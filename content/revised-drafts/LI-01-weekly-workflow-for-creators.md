## Slide 1 — Cover
A Repeatable Weekly Content Workflow for Creators

## Slide 2 — Start with one audience need
Choose one question your audience genuinely wants answered.

## Slide 3 — Select a source
Use an original podcast segment, video, interview, article, or lesson you have permission to reuse.

## Slide 4 — Extract one useful idea
Write the main takeaway in a single sentence before choosing a format.

## Slide 5 — Adapt thoughtfully
Turn the idea into a post, carousel, or short video. Adjust the structure for each format instead of copying blindly.

## Slide 6 — Review and schedule
Check facts, permissions, captions, links, and approval status before publishing.

## Caption
For a creator or small content team, a repeatable workflow starts with one useful audience need—not a pile of disconnected post ideas. Choose a source, extract a clear lesson, adapt it to the format, and review it before publishing.
